1. Introduction

Hooter is a multi-channel Order and Inventory Management System (OMS) designed for brands and marketplace sellers operating across multiple product categories. Our platform enables authorized businesses to manage products, inventory, orders, shipping, pricing, invoicing, and marketplace financial reconciliation through a centralized dashboard.

We recognize that marketplace information may contain sensitive commercial information as well as customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII). Protecting this information is a fundamental part of our platform design and operational procedures.

2. Scope of this Policy

This policy applies to:

  • Authorized sellers using Hooter.
  • Amazon SP-API information processed by Hooter.
  • Customer shipping and billing information required for fulfillment.
  • Marketplace operational and financial records.
  • Employees and administrators who access production systems.
Important: Hooter only accesses Amazon information after the seller explicitly authorizes the application through Amazon's OAuth authorization process.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide requested services.

4. Amazon SP-API Information

Hooter uses Amazon SP-API only for authorized operational purposes.

Product & Inventory

  • Import and synchronize listings
  • Update inventory quantities
  • Maintain centralized catalog information

Orders & Fulfillment

  • Import merchant fulfilled orders
  • Retrieve shipping labels
  • Retrieve tax invoices
  • Submit shipment confirmations and tracking

Financial Operations

  • Settlement reconciliation
  • Marketplace transaction history
  • Financial reporting for sellers

5. How We Use Information

Information collected through Hooter is used exclusively to provide services requested by the authorized seller.

  • Synchronize products across connected marketplaces.
  • Maintain accurate inventory levels.
  • Manage order processing workflows.
  • Generate and print shipping labels.
  • Generate tax invoices.
  • Update shipment status and tracking information.
  • Reconcile marketplace payouts with business records.
Hooter does not use Amazon customer information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or unrelated marketing activities.

6. Legal Basis & Authorization

Hooter processes Amazon information only after explicit authorization from the seller.

  • OAuth authorization is required before any Amazon data is accessed.
  • Sellers may revoke access at any time through Amazon or Hooter.
  • Access tokens are stored securely and refreshed only when required.

7. Data Storage

Production data is stored on secured infrastructure with network isolation.

  • Databases are not publicly accessible.
  • Internal services communicate over private application networks.
  • Public traffic is protected using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Administrative interfaces are restricted to authorized personnel.

8. Encryption & Information Security

Hooter applies multiple layers of security controls to protect marketplace information.

ControlImplementation
Data in TransitHTTPS with TLS encryption
Data at RestEncryption for sensitive customer information
AuthenticationUnique authenticated user accounts
AuthorizationRole-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Audit LoggingAdministrative and privileged access events
Least PrivilegeMinimum permissions required for each role
Customer Personally Identifiable Information is accessed only when required to fulfill merchant operations such as shipping label generation, tax invoicing, and order fulfillment.

9. Employee Identification & Access Control

Every employee is individually identified using a unique authenticated account.

  • No shared employee accounts are permitted.
  • Role-based permissions restrict access to business responsibilities.
  • Administrative privileges are limited to authorized personnel.
  • Access is reviewed periodically and revoked immediately when no longer required.

Hooter follows the principle of least privilege across all production systems.

10. Sharing of Information

Hooter never sells or rents Amazon information.

Information is shared only in the following circumstances:

  • To provide services explicitly requested by the seller.
  • With service providers necessary for infrastructure operation.
  • When required by applicable law or valid legal process.
  • With Amazon as part of authorized SP-API communication.

11. Data Retention & Secure Disposal

Marketplace information is retained only as long as necessary for operational, contractual, or legal purposes.

Data TypeRetention
Operational OrdersUntil business purpose is fulfilled
Shipping DocumentsAs required for fulfillment and compliance
Financial RecordsAccording to applicable accounting requirements
Access LogsSecurity monitoring period

When retention expires, information is securely deleted from active systems and removed according to internal disposal procedures.

12. Security Monitoring & Incident Response

Hooter continuously monitors production infrastructure for unauthorized access attempts and privileged account activity.

  • Authentication events are logged.
  • Administrative actions are auditable.
  • Suspicious activity is investigated by authorized administrators.
  • Confirmed incidents follow documented containment and recovery procedures.

Where required by Amazon SP-API security requirements, Amazon will be notified of applicable security incidents within the required timeframe.

13. Seller Rights

Authorized sellers maintain control over their marketplace connection.

  • Connect or disconnect Amazon authorization.
  • Request deletion of applicable business data.
  • Export operational information where supported.
  • Review marketplace synchronization status.

14. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy & Data Handling Policy to reflect changes in legal requirements, security practices, or platform functionality.

The effective date at the top of this document will indicate the latest revision.
